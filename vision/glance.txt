- Full-screen, map-centric flight-tracking interface.
- Split into:
  - **Large main map canvas** on the left (~73–75% width).
  - **Fixed dark right sidebar** on the right (~25–27% width).
- No conventional full-width application header; instead, the map has **floating controls and a compact top-center banner**.

### Main canvas / map region
- Occupies nearly the entire left side and extends from top to bottom.
- Displays a geographic basemap centered on the New York / Long Island / New Jersey / Connecticut area.
- The map is visibly **darkened/desaturated by an overlay**, making aircraft markers and UI controls stand out.
- Contains:
  - Many multicolored aircraft icons.
  - A few black/gray special aircraft or non-aircraft-looking markers.
  - Fine map labels, roads, coastlines, borders, and city names.
  - Bottom-left map scale.
  - Bottom-center rainbow altitude legend.

  - Zoom controls at lower-right of map.

## 2. Color estimates

These are visual approximations from the screenshot rather than sampled source values.

### (a) Main map/canvas background
The map is a standard muted street/geographic map with a dark translucent overlay.

- **Dominant water / map field:** approximately `#5F7C80` to `#68868A`
- **Darkened land areas:** approximately `#627465` to `#6F806E`
- **Dark map overlay tint:** likely a semitransparent charcoal/blue-gray, visually similar to `rgba(22, 34, 37, 0.25–0.40)`
- **Road/boundary lines:** faded beige-gray / muted brown, around `#9B9A7A` and `#77725E`

The visual result is a subdued gray-green-blue map, designed to prioritize brightly colored aircraft.

### (b) Side-panel/card backgrounds
- **Primary right sidebar background:** near `#2B2B2B` / `#2E2E2E`
- **Sidebar section / tab-strip background:** around `#252525` to `#303030`
- **Input backgrounds:** very dark gray, roughly `#202020` or `#1D1D1D`
- **Table body rows:** blue-gray, approximately `#315267` to `#365B70`
- **Table header:** deep muted blue, around `#1E4D67`
- **Alternating table row variation:** subtly darker/lighter blue-grays, approximately `#2C4E62` and `#36596D`

### (c) Primary accent color
The main UI accent is a medium-to-dark cyan-blue:

- **Primary accent blue:** approximately `#00629A`, `#006B9F`, or `#0875A8`
- Used for:
  - Active `Search` tab.
  - Map toolbar buttons.
  - Table header cells.
  - Certain map controls.

A lighter active/hover variation may be around `#1685B8`.

### (d) Semantic status colors
There is no obvious conventional alert banner, but semantic-like colors are visible in map markers and table indicator strips.

- **Success / green:** `#00C848` to `#17D33A`
  - Used for aircraft icons and small table status/category marks.
- **Warning / yellow:** `#F4D300` to `#FFD500`
  - Used for aircraft icons and altitude legend.
- **Orange:** `#FF8A00` to `#FF9800`

  - Prominent aircraft color family, likely representing a value range rather than error.
- **Cyan:** `#00C6DA` to `#00D6E9`
  - Aircraft icon colors / map data classification.

### (e) Text colors
- **Primary text:** approximately `#E5E5E5` to `#F2F2F2`
  - Headings, values, table content, major labels.
- **Secondary/muted text:** approximately `#B5B5B5` to `#C4C4C4`

## 3. Border style

### Right sidebar
- Appears to have **no strongly visible outer border**; separation from the map is primarily through the abrupt dark background shift.
- May have a thin left divider around:
  - `#1B1B1B` or `#444444`
  - roughly **1 px**
- Corners: none; the panel is flush to the viewport edges.
- No obvious shadow; it reads as a fixed docked panel.

### Sidebar sections / tab areas
- Mostly separated by spacing, darker strip backgrounds, or very subtle lines.
- Likely **1 px solid separators** in dark gray (`#414141`, `#1C1C1C`, or similar).
- Square corners; no card-like floating treatment.
- No visible glow or significant box shadow.

### Table
- Dense grid styling.
- Cells have thin dividers:
  - approximately **1 px**
  - blue/dark-blue lines around `#19445B` or `#1A3E51`
- Header cells have clear grid boundaries.
- Square corners.
- No shadow.

### Buttons
- Compact rectangular controls.
- Typical button style:
  - Background: dark gray (`#3B3B3B` / `#444444`) or active blue (`#006B9F`).
  - Border: subtle **1 px solid**, approximately `#555555` on gray buttons or `#0A527B` on blue buttons.
  - Radius: effectively **0–2 px**; nearly square.
- Search/tab button active state is blue-filled rather than outlined.
- No glow; contrast comes from fill color.

### Inputs
- Dark, flat rectangular fields.
- Likely:
  - Background `#1D1D1D`–`#252525`
  - Border: **1 px solid** `#555555` / `#606060`
  - Radius: 0–2 px
- Inputs are short in height and optimized for dense operational use.

### Floating map controls
- Square blue icon buttons have:
  - Solid blue fill (`#00679A`-ish).
  - Fine dark borders / separators.
  - Nearly square corners, perhaps 1–2 px radius.
- The “Try the new UI” banner has a slightly more rounded shape:
  - Roughly **3–5 px radius**
  - Blue fill.
  - Dark edge or subtle shadow to remain legible on the map.
- No soft/glassmorphism effect; styling is utilitarian.

### Aircraft markers
- Aircraft icons have dark outlines, likely:
  - 1–2 px black/dark charcoal stroke (`#17232A`).
- The outline is important for visibility against both land and water.
- Some black icons appear to use pale internal detailing or contour lines.

## 5. Typography and visual style

### Font character
- Primarily a **compact sans-serif UI font**, likely Arial, Helvetica, Roboto, or a system sans stack.
- It does not appear monospaced overall.
- The table’s numeric data may use tabular numerals, but the font itself appears proportional sans-serif.

### Typography hierarchy
- **Brand / “Airplanes.live”:**
  - Larger, bold or semi-bold.
  - Underlined.
  - Light gray/white.
- **Section labels and links:**
  - Small-to-medium size.
  - Bold or semibold in places.
- **Control labels:**
  - Very compact, around 10–12 px apparent size.
- **Table text:**
  - Very dense, likely 9–11 px.
  - High information density with minimal padding.
- **Map labels:**
  - Small muted geographic labeling inherited from map tiles.

### Density
- Strongly **operations-dashboard / data-dense** design.
- Minimal whitespace in the sidebar.
- Compact buttons, tabs, fields, and table rows.
- Designed for scanning many flights at once rather than for spacious consumer
